# 农夫步行动画

角色外观和像素画风沿用原有农夫。两张通过内置 ImageGen 生成的透明稿保存在 `assets/pixel/v0.8.7/source/`，分别是 `farmer-walk.png` 和 `farmer-side-walk.png`。构建时提取站立、两次着地和两次过步姿势，按脚底对齐，以最近邻缩小。左侧步行动作由右侧镜像得到，正面和背面根据实际抬脚关系编排动作。

## 三方向原稿提示词

> Use case: stylized-concept. Make a new production sprite sheet for the identical chibi pixel-art female farmer shown in the attached image. Use the attachment as a character and pixel-art style reference only; replace the running-like frozen stances with a real two-step walk. Preserve her broad tan hat with blue band, red-brown braided hair, white rolled sleeves, blue overalls, brown boots and small leather bag; preserve the slightly elevated orthographic camera, warm sunlight and fine pixel texture. Output exactly 15 isolated poses in an evenly spaced 5 columns × 3 rows rectangular grid. Row 1: faces camera, moves toward us. Row 2: side view faces strictly RIGHT in all five cells. Row 3: back view faces away from camera in all five cells. Within EACH row the five columns represent, in order: [0 neutral standing upright, BOTH boots flat and side by side under body]; [1 CONTACT A, the left foot clearly planted forward, right foot clearly behind, opposite arm swing]; [2 PASSING A, feet visibly close together beneath hips, right knee lifted and passing the planted foot]; [3 CONTACT B, the right foot clearly planted forward and left foot clearly behind, arm positions reversed]; [4 PASSING B, feet again close under hips with left knee lifted and passing]. Deliberately make columns 1 and 3 visibly alternate the leg overlap and arm swing, and make columns 2 and 4 distinctly narrower than the contact poses. In side view both planted stride silhouettes point right, with near and far boots identifiable from their shading, and passing poses have ankles closer than half of the contact stride. For front and back views, use conspicuously alternating left/right ground-level boot contacts. Centre each whole sprite in its cell with consistent scale and ground contact baseline. Every sprite is completely isolated by large transparent gutters, use only the middle 75% of each cell and leave all outer borders transparent. True transparent RGBA background, no floor, no shadows outside characters, no grid lines, no text, no props, no additional poses.

## 侧向补画提示词

> This attached sprite sheet is the exact character and pixel art style reference. Create a replacement right-facing side-view WALK ONLY sprite sheet, with four isolated full-body poses in a 2×2 grid on transparent RGBA. Match the side-facing (middle-row) character closely: blue-banded straw hat, red-brown braid, white sleeves, blue overalls, tan brown boots, side satchel, warm classic pixel art. Make the leg positions unmistakably different. IMPORTANT: all four poses face RIGHT, right is forward, and the leg facing the viewer (near leg) is lighter and drawn IN FRONT of the far leg; the far boot and trouser leg stay a dark shadow brown/navy. Do not mirror the entire character. TOP LEFT: near leg straight forward to the right with its light boot contacting the ground, far leg extended backward to the left with a dark boot, near hand swinging behind the torso. TOP RIGHT: narrow passing pose, near light boot planted under the hips with the dark far knee and boot LIFTED and passing just ahead, near hand swings inward. BOTTOM LEFT: opposite contact, dark far leg straight forward to the RIGHT with its dark boot on the ground; the brighter near leg visibly extends BACK to the LEFT, near hand swings forward. BOTTOM RIGHT: narrow opposite passing pose, dark far boot planted under hips, light near leg lifts forward with its light boot in FRONT of the dark leg; near hand sweeps back. The forward boot must distinctly alternate LIGHT, then DARK in the two contact cells. All sprites complete and aligned with generous transparent gutters. No backdrop, outlines around cells, diagram markings, text, or any objects besides the four sprites.
